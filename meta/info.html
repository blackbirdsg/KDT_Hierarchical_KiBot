KiCad 10 Compatibility - Hierarchical Design Template

Development fork of Vincent Nguyen's KiCad 8/9 template for hierarchical projects and KiBot documentation. Targets KiCad 10; local full-output trial used KiCad 10.0.5 and KiBot 1.9.1. Experimental support: board validation and KiCad 10 CI qualification remain open.
